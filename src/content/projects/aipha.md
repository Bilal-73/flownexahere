## Problem

Fitness users jump between separate apps, search results, calorie notes and workout lists. Planning becomes inconsistent and hard to keep up, especially for people who just want simple daily direction.

## What we built

AIPHA is a conversational assistant for fitness, nutrition and daily wellness. Users describe a goal, such as weight loss, and get customised workout recommendations, diet suggestions and practical next steps in one readable answer. They can then refine the plan with follow-up questions.

The assistant presents routines, meals and habit support in structured blocks that are easy to scan on phones and tablets.

### Key features

- **Conversational guidance:** ask health and fitness questions in a chat and get practical, goal-based answers.
- **Workout recommendations:** plans for fat loss, strength, cardio and full-body training based on the user's goals.
- **Meal suggestions:** calorie-conscious meals with balanced protein and carbs.
- **Personal goal support:** plans adapt to age, weight-loss goals, lifestyle and daily activity.
- **Smart reminders:** designed to support reminders for hydration, meals, workouts and habits.
- **Readable advice:** structured answers so users understand the advice before acting on it.

## How it works

1. The user asks a fitness, diet or wellness question in the chat.
2. The assistant interprets goals such as weight loss, activity level or food preferences.
3. AIPHA returns a structured plan with workout and diet recommendations.
4. The user continues the conversation to refine routines, meals and habits.

## Tech

- **AI:** conversational AI, recommendation logic, prompt workflows
- **Product:** mobile-first assistant, chat UI, structured responses
- **Wellness scope:** workout planning, diet guidance, daily habits
- **Experience:** responsive screens for phone and tablet

## Result

AIPHA is a demo build. It is designed to:

- give one simple place to get fitness and nutrition guidance;
- cut the time spent searching for workouts, diet ideas and routines;
- support personalised daily planning;
- provide a base for future tracking, reminders and deeper personalisation.

AIPHA offers general wellness information, not medical advice.
