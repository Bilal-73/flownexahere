## Problem

Clinics often rely on scattered scheduling notes, manual follow-ups and disconnected patient information. That causes delays, missed reminders and extra work for front-desk and medical staff.

## What we built

Clinify is a clinic operations hub that brings appointment handling, patient visibility, doctor management, notifications and AI-driven assistance into one responsive dashboard.

The dashboard gives a clear view of core operations: total patients, today's appointments, completed appointments, patient records and the medical team. It is designed for clinics that want a cleaner way to organise daily work, reduce manual coordination and prepare for automated patient communication.

### Key features

- **Clinic dashboard:** patient totals, today's appointments, completed appointments and team status in one place.
- **Appointment booking:** workflows to schedule visits and manage appointment activity.
- **Patient management:** patient records and status views for a clearer operational picture.
- **Doctor directory:** medical team cards with specialties and availability.
- **Notifications:** automated reminders and updates to reduce missed appointments and manual follow-up.
- **AI assistance:** support for patient handling, admin questions and faster responses.

## How it works

1. Staff open the dashboard to review patients, appointments and team availability.
2. New appointments are booked and assigned to the right doctor or service.
3. Patient records and appointment statuses are updated in the dashboard.
4. Automated notifications remind patients and staff about upcoming or changed appointments.
5. AI assistance handles routine questions and guides staff through workflows.
6. Managers review completed appointments and clinic activity.

## Tech

- **Frontend:** responsive clinic dashboard
- **Automation:** appointment reminders, notifications and workflow triggers (n8n)
- **Core modules:** patients, doctors, appointments, clinic dashboard
- **AI scope:** admin assistance, patient guidance, workflow support

## Result

Clinify is a demo build. It is designed to:

- give clinics one dashboard for patients, appointments and doctors;
- reduce manual follow-up with notification-ready workflows;
- help staff coordinate appointments more consistently;
- provide a base for AI-assisted patient support.
